## Production notes

- Capture from a physical Pixel with `adb shell screenrecord` after APK install.
- Prefer real MapLibre motion over stock footage.
- Do **not** show active scanning of third-party infrastructure.
- Brand colors: `#0A0A0A` background, `#E8B84A` accent, sparse chrome.
